# PRD

## Project Name

CabinFlow

## User

Airline passengers and cabin crew

## Customer

Sold to seatback IFE manufacturers (e.g. Panasonic Avionics) as a module on their platform.

## Problem

Airplane cabin operations are inefficient and lead to unnecesary cabin movement during flight times. We can use a simple mobile application that connects to the on screen tablets to help with bathroom queues, assistance, better flight info, and snack services so that cabin operations are smoother.

## Core User Story

As a passenger on the flight I can click on the bathroom section to reserve a place in line to go to the bathroom and can choose to reserve my spot in line. I will be able to see when it is my turn and how many people are in front of me. I can also go to flight info part to see expected turbulence information and other flight status and information about arriving airport. I can also go to the snack services section to see what drinks and snacks are available and make a request for when snack services start. Finally, I can use the assistance button to make a request. As a flight attendant I would be able to see assistance requests, see bathroom lines, and see snack selections

## Features

1. Login screen to choose passenger or flight attendant
2. Passenger can click on bathroom line section to reserce spot in line and see line info
3. Flight attendants can see bathroom line information and has power to double check whos turn it is
4. Flight attendant can input snack cart items to snack screen
5. Passengers can click on what drink and snack they prefer
6. Flight attendants can see passengers drink selection to know what they want ahead of time
7. Passengers can click assistance and select what type of assistance they want along with the request
8. Flight attendants can see the requests and manage them accordingly
9. Passengers can see flight information such as expected turbulence and destination airport information

## Out of Scope

1. Connecting with ground services or stuff like booking hotels or cars from airplane
2. communication between different passengers
3. displaying info about people in front of passenger or displaying other people's preference
4. connecting to cockpit for readings on turbulence or altitude, etc

## Data We Store

Only data about preferences not about people, for example commonly wanted snacks and drinks and how often people use assitance and what type of assistance
